# Fitness photograph

Asset: `apps/web/public/images/training-studio.webp`

Generated using the built-in image generation tool for this redesign, then resized to 1200 × 800 and encoded as WebP (66,278 bytes). It was used in the first design and is retained as an unused asset. The current sign-in surface is deliberately typographic.

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: understated editorial photograph for a premium fitness coaching app sign-in screen. Landscape 3:2. A quiet contemporary training studio, close low angle of a single matte graphite kettlebell on a pale grey textured floor, soft morning daylight from tall windows making a long diagonal shadow, just a small out-of-focus sage green exercise mat at the edge. Restrained architectural composition with generous uncluttered negative space in the upper half. Natural photographic material texture, calm sophisticated wellness feel, subtle film grain, cool neutral whites and graphite with muted green. No people, no text, no logos, no interface, no icons, no watermark. High quality art-directed real photography, not a 3d render.
